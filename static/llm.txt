# Kijeka Engineers Private Limited

> India's premier B2B manufacturer and exporter of Material Handling Equipment (MHE) since 1980.

## Overview
Kijeka Engineers Private Limited, headquartered in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India, designs, manufactures, and exports premium material handling equipment, custom warehouse solutions, lifting accessories, and specialized B2B handling machines.

## Product Categories
- [Aerial Work Platforms](https://www.kijeka.com/aerial-work-platform): Double mast and single mast aerial lifts.
- [Aluminium Ladders](https://www.kijeka.com/aluminium-ladders): Staircases, extension, step, and tower ladders.
- [Chemical Pumps & Accessories](https://www.kijeka.com/chemical-pumps-acces): AODD, gear, plunger, and electric IBC drum pumps.
- [Cranes & Lifting Accessories](https://www.kijeka.com/cranes-lifting-accessories): Wire rope hoists, EOT cranes, and forklift mounted accessories.
- [Dock Levellers & Ramp](https://www.kijeka.com/dock-levellers-dock-ramp): B2B dock levelers and portable yard ramps.
- [Drum Equipments](https://www.kijeka.com/drum-equipments): Lifters, tilters, dollies, storage racks, and trucks.
- [Lifts & Scissor Platforms](https://www.kijeka.com/hydraulic-scissor-lift-platform): Scissor lift tables, goods lifts, and self-propelled platforms.
- [Pallet Trucks](https://www.kijeka.com/pallet-trucks): Hydraulic hand, electric, high-lift, and customized pallet trucks.
- [Wheels, Casters & Springs](https://www.kijeka.com/wheels-casters): Polyurethane traction wheels, casters, and rebonding services.
